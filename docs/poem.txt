Sun‑kissed waves at Hua Hin sway in molten bright  
Bangkok’s bustling streets pulse beneath a humid dream, then take flight  
Aalborg’s twilight drapes the harbor in a cool, silvery night  
I wander between them, heart a compass seeking its gentle height
