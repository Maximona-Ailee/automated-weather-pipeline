The golden hush of sunrise drapes Hua Hin in a soft glow  
Bangkok’s bustling streets sway with humid whispers that gently flow  
Aalborg’s distant harbor sighs under pale clouds, a quiet snow  
I wander through these moods, letting the world decide its show
