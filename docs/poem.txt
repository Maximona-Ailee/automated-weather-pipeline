The golden heat drapes Hua Hin like a silk shawl that sways  
In bustling Bangkok, warm droplets tap the pavement, urging a gentle play  
Far north in Aalborg, crisp breath hovers, and the fierce wind sings in muted gray  
Which whisper calls your heart to linger, where will you drift away?
