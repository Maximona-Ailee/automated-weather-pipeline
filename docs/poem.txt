Golden haze drapes Hua Hin’s lazy coast, a sigh that glow  
Bangkok hums beneath a thick sweet steam, its pulse a restless flow  
Aalborg shivers under a hushed silver veil, each breath a frosted snow  
Which whispering wind will carry my wandering heart to show
