Sunlit silk drapes Hua Hin’s coast, where lazy breezes sing  
Bangkok sways beneath a humid veil, the wind a soft sigh  
Aalborg shivers under pale clouds, the chill whispering lull  
Which cradle of sky will cradle my heart
